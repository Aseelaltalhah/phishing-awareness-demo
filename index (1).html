The scenario illustrates three techniques that attackers may combine:

  1. Brand impersonation: a familiar learning provider can make an unexpected request appear credible.
  2. Deadline pressure: a supposed last chance to activate a course encourages quick clicks.
  3. Lookalike domains: characters from different alphabets can visually resemble letters in a trusted web address.

What to do instead: open your learning platform from a known bookmark or trusted company portal, check the full sender address and destination domain, and report suspicious messages to your security team.
